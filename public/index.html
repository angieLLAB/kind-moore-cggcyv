Who we are. Learnership Lab (Pty) Ltd, 1 Johanna Street, Florentia, Alberton, 1449 (Reg. 2017/096491/07). Our Information Officer is Angie Booysen: angie@learnershiplab.co.za, 079 520 0186.

What we collect. Your name, ID or alternative ID number, date of birth, contact and address details, language, nationality, education and employment details, and your signature. We also collect population group and disability status, which are special personal information.

Why. To enrol you in a learnership or qualification, deliver and assess your training, and report to SAQA, the SETA and QCTO as required. We collect population group and disability status because we are required to report them to SAQA, the SETA and QCTO. Giving us this information is voluntary, but we cannot enrol you without it.

Who receives it. SAQA, the SETA, QCTO and your employer; and service providers who handle it for us: Supabase (database), Netlify (website hosting), Resend and Microsoft 365 (email and documents). Our staff may also use an external AI service (Anthropic) to read ID documents and certificates you provide.

Where it is stored. Your information is stored on servers in Ireland, and some of our providers are outside South Africa.

How long we keep it. For 5 years after your training ends.

Your rights. You may ask to see, correct or delete your information (subject to our legal record-keeping duties), and you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa), inforegulator.org.za.
